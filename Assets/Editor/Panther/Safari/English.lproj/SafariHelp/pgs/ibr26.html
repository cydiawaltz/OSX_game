Using your own style sheet

Cascading Style Sheets (CSS) let you specify how elements in a webpage are displayed. Safari lets you use your own style sheet in addition to any style sheets specified by the page.

Choose Preferences from the Safari menu, then click Advanced. Choose Other from the Style Sheet pop-up menu. Locate the style sheet you want to use, select it, and click Choose.

For more information about Cascading Style Sheets, visit the World Wide Web Consortium. Look for "CSS" in the "W3C A to Z" contents list.
