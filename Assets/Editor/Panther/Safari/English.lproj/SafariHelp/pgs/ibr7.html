Opening Rendezvous websites

Safari uses Rendezvous to discover websites that are available on your local network or subnet.

To see available Rendezvous websites, click the bookmarks library icon in the bookmarks bar and then select Rendezvous in the Collections column.

If you have a printer available using Rendezvous, you might see it listed as a Rendezvous website. You can open the website to configure the printer.

You can add Rendezvous websites to the bookmarks bar and the Bookmarks menu by using the Bookmarks pane of Safari preferences. You can also choose to show Rendezvous websites in Bookmarks Library.
