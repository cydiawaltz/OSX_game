Opening home pages from your Address Book

In Safari, you can easily open websites for businesses or individuals listed in your Address Book.

In Address Book, select a card and enter the address for the website in the "home page" field of the card.

To see the home pagesin Safari, choose Show All Bookmarks from the Bookmarks menu and select Address Book. Double-click a page to open it.

Using the Bookmarks pane of Safari preferences, you can add Address Book home pages to the bookmarks bar and the Bookmarks menu. You can also choose to show Address Book home pages in Bookmarks Library.
