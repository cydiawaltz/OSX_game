Seeing which plug-ins are available to Safari

Safari works with the plug-ins installed with Mac OS X that support Java, QuickTime, and Shockwave Flash content. It also works with plug-ins that conform to the Netscape standard plug-in archictecture, such as the Macromedia Shockwave Player plug-in (named Shockwave for Director).

To see the plug-ins available to Safari, choose Installed Plug-ins from the Help menu.
