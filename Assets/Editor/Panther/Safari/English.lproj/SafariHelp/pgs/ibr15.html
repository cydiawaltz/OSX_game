Organizing your bookmarks

In the Bookmarks Library, you can organize your collection of bookmarks, including the bookmarks in the bookmarks bar and Bookmarks menu.

You cannot organize the items in the Address Book, Rendezvous, or History collections. You can delete items from the History collection.

To see the Bookmarks Library, click the icon at the left end of the bookmarks bar.

In the Bookmarks Library, the left column shows your collections of bookmarks, including the Bookmarks Bar, Bookmarks Menu, Address Book, Rendezvous, and History collections.

The right column shows the names and addresses of bookmarks. It also shows folders that contain other bookmarks.

To see the contents of a collection, click it.

To move a bookmark to a collection, locate the bookmark and drag its icon to the name of the collection. To copy the bookmark to the collection, hold down the Option key as you drag the icon. You can move or copy bookmarks to folders in a collection the same way.

To create a new collection, click the Add (+) button at the bottom of the Collections column. To add a folder to a collection, select the collection and click the Add (+) button at the bottom of the Bookmark column.

To arrange collections, drag them up or down. To add a collection to another collection, drag the icon of the collection you want to add to the collection you want to add it to.

To arrange folders or bookmarks in a collection, select the collection, then drag the icons up or down.

To change the name of an item, select the name and press Enter. Type the new name, then press Return. You can also change the address of a bookmark.

To remove a collection, a folder in a collection, or a bookmark, select the item and press Delete.
