Returning to your Google search results

When you click a link in a Google search results page, you'll see a SnapBack button in the Google search box. You can use this SnapBack button to return to your search results, no matter where you browse to.

To return to the page of search results you were viewing most recently, click the SnapBack button in the Google search box.

If you perform Google searches in different windows, the Google SnapBack button in each window returns you to the search results for that window.

If you click the SnapBack button in the Google search box, Safari removes the SnapBack button from the address box, if there is one.
