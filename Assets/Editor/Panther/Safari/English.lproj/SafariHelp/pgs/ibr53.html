Clearing the Downloads window

As you download items from webpages, they appear in the Downloads window. If you have too many items in the window, you can clear it. You can also set how you want Safari to remove items from the Downloads window.

To clear the Downloads window, make the window active or choose Downloads from the Window menu. Click the Clear button at the bottom of the window.

If you want to set when Safari removes items from the window, choose Preferences from the Safari menu, then choose a command from the "Remove download list items" pop-up menu.

Clearing the Downloads window does not clear files that are being downloaded.
