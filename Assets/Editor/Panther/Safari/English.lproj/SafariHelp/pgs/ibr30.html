Managing cookies

Websites store "cookies" on your computer to facilitate transactions with the site. In most cases, cookies are harmless. For some websites, you must allow cookies to be accepted in order to use the site.

If you are concerned about cookies, you can set how you want Safari to handle them.

Choose Preferences from the Safari menu and click Security. Select Never if you don't want to store any cookies on your computer. This may prevent you from using some websites.

Click Always to accept and remember all cookies.

Click "Only from sites you navigate to" to prevent storing cookies that come from websites other than those you open. Select this option to prevent advertisers on websites you visit from storing cookies on your computer.

To see the cookies stored on your computer, click Show Cookies. To remove a cookie, select it in the list and click Delete.

If you want to stop accepting cookies, you should also delete all cookies after you select Never in the Security pane.

You may need to temporarily accept cookies to open a page. Select Always to accept the cookies and open the page. You can then turn off cookies. You may want to delete cookies after you have viewed the page.
