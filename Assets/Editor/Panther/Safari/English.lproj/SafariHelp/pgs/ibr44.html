Browsing webpages in tabs

If you turn on tabbed browsing, you can open webpages in tabs rather than replacing the content of the current window or opening a separate window.

Browsing webpages using tabs makes it easier to open several pages at once because you don't need to open several windows.

When you open a page in a tab, the tab bar appears below the bookmarks bar. The name of the page appears in a tab in the tab bar.

You can also open a collection of bookmarks in tabs. While you view one page, the others can be loading.

To turn on tabbed browsing, choose Preferences from the Safari menu and click Tab. Select Enable Tabbed Browsing.

To open a link or bookmark in the bookmarks bar in a new tab, hold down the Command key and click the item, or hold down the Control key, click the item, and choose Open in New Tab from the shortcut menu.

To open a bookmark collection in the bookmarks bar or a folder of bookmarks in the Bookmarks Library in tabs, hold down the Control key, click the collection or folder, and choose Open in Tabs from the shortcut menu.

If you select the Auto-Tab checkbox for a folder in the bookmarks bar, then you can open all the bookmarks in the folder in tabs by clicking the folder name in the bookmarks bar.

To open a webpage in a new tab, choose New Tab from the File menu and type the address for the page.

To close a tab, click the close button in the tab.

To show a webpage that is in a tab and close all other tabs, hold down the Option key and click the close box in the tab you want to remain open.

When you click a link in a page you are viewing in a tab, the page opens in the current tab. Click the Back and Forward buttons in the address bar to scan pages you have opened in a tab.

If you choose a page from the History or Bookmarks menu, the page opens in the current tab.

When you turn on tabbed browsing, the shortcuts for opening windows change. For example, to open a page in a new window, hold down the Command and Option key and click a link. The list at the bottom of the Tab pane shows you what shortcuts to use depending on how you have set up tabbed browsing.

If you select to open links from other applications in the current window and enable tabbed browsing, the page opens in a new tab in the current window.
