Selecting your home page

Your "home page" is the page that usually opens when you open Safari, open a new window, or choose Home from the History menu. You can select any page as your home page.

Open a webpage in Safari, then choose Preferences from the Safari menu and click General. Click Set to Current Page.

You can also type the address for the page you want to use in the "Home page" box.

This also changes your home page setting in Internet preferences.

If you want new windows to open with the home page, choose Home Page from the "New windows open with" pop-up menu.
