Returning to your home page

Your "home page" is the webpage you see when you open Safari or open a new window in Safari. Safari is set to open the home page specified in Internet preferences.

To return directly to your home page, choose Home from the History menu.

To select a new home page or change what appears in new Safari windows, choose Preferences from the Safari menu then click General.

When you click a link in your home page, you'll see the SnapBack button at the right of the address box. After you have clicked other links, you can click the SnapBack button to return to your home page.
