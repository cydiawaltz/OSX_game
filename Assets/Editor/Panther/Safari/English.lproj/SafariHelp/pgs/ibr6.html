Using Google to search the Internet

Safari provides a Google search box in the address bar so you can use Google to search the Internet.

Click the Google search box and type a word or phrase that describes what you're looking for, then press Return.

Click a link in the list of search results to open a webpage.

To return to the last page of Google search results you were viewing, click the SnapBack button in the Google search box.

To repeat a previous search, click the magnifying glass in the Google search box and choose the search term from the pop-up menu.

When you perform a new Google search or return to your last Google search results page, Safari removes the SnapBack button from the address box.
