Controlling the content loaded by a webpage

You can control what content is loaded when you open a webpage. You may want to prevent pages from loading certain types of content for security reasons or because you find the content annoying.

To prevent content from being loaded, choose Preferences from the Safari menu and click Security. Then deselect the content you don't want to load.

If you want to block pop-up windows, select the checkbox. You can also choose Block Pop-up Windows from the Safari menu.

You can also select whether you want to accept cookies and which cookies to accept.

Webpages use plug-ins, Java, and JavaScript to display information. If you turn off these features, you may not be able to see some of the page's content.
