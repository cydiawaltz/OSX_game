Choosing what appears in a new window

You can choose what appears in a new window when you open it in Safari.

Choose Preferences from the Safari menu and click General. Then choose a setting from the "New windows open with" pop-up menu.

Choose Home Page to open the webpage you have specified as your home page.

Choose Same Page to open the page that is open in the current active window.

If you don't want to open any page, choose Empty Page.

Choose Bookmarks to display the Bookmarks Library.

Safari marks the page opened in the new window as the SnapBack page for the window.
