A message says a plug-in is not available

Safari works with all standard plug-ins. If you are experiencing a problem with a plug-in, please let Apple know by clicking the bug icon in the address bar.

Safari works with the plug-ins installed with Mac OS X that support Java, QuickTime, and Shockwave Flash. It also works with plug-ins that conform to the Netscape standard plug-in archictecture, such as the Macromedia Shockwave Player plug-in (named Shockwave for Director).
